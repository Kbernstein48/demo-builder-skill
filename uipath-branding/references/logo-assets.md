## Filename Metadata

The filenames encode intended use:

- `digital`: use for screens, web, apps, email, video, social, and other RGB workflows.
- `print`: use for print production and CMYK workflows.
- `rgb`: RGB color model for digital.
- `cmyk`: CMYK color model for print.
- `b`: black logo; use on white or light backgrounds when contrast is sufficient.
- `w`: white-out logo; use on colored, dark, or photography backgrounds with clear contrast.
- `o`: all-orange logo; digital-only functional exception. Do not use in print.
- `corporate-logo`: corporate logo naming used by the orange digital assets in this package.

## Selection Rules

- Prefer SVG for scalable digital UI, web, and app work when vector support is available.
- Use PNG for quick raster placement in digital assets, prototypes, docs, and slides. Bundled PNGs are transparent RGBA at 1290 x 420.
- Use AI or EPS for designer-controlled production workflows.
- Use print CMYK AI/EPS files for print production. The package includes print black and print white variants only.
- Use the white-out logo on colored, dark, or photo backgrounds.
- Use the black logo on white or light backgrounds.
- Use the orange logo only in digital contexts, especially adaptive environments such as email signatures. Use sparingly.
- Do not crop, stretch, recolor, retype, trace, flatten into a new mark, or otherwise modify these files.
- Do not create logo lockups from these files. Use Creative Studio-provided lockups only.
