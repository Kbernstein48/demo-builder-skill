## Usage Notes

- Prefer the contact sheets for quick orientation.
- Open individual examples when building or reviewing a specific artifact type.
- Use the full PDF at `references/source/uipath-brand-book-v3.3.pdf` when page-level details or official context are needed.
- Do not treat rendered examples as production logo, icon, Otto, or template assets. Use official Brand Center assets for final production work.
