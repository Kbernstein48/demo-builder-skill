# UiPath Branding

## Overview

Use this skill to make work feel unmistakably UiPath: bold, human, direct, structured, and high contrast. The guidance is distilled from the UiPath Brand Book V3.3 and is meant for practical creation and review across UI, web, slides, documents, campaigns, and other brand touchpoints.

## Workflow

1. Determine the output type: UI/web, slide deck, document, social asset, report, event creative, campaign, photography-led layout, or brand review.
2. Read [references/brand-requirements.md](references/brand-requirements.md) when exact tokens, logo rules, type rules, photography rules, or brand restrictions matter.
3. Read [references/logo-assets.md](references/logo-assets.md) before placing a UiPath logo, selecting a white/black/orange variant, or choosing between digital RGB and print CMYK assets.
4. Read [references/icon-assets.md](references/icon-assets.md) before placing a UiPath icon. Use [references/icon-index.md](references/icon-index.md) or [references/icon-index.json](references/icon-index.json) to search all available white and dark-grey 96 px SVG icons by name, keyword, category, and variant path.
5. Read [references/design-language.md](references/design-language.md) when creating a net-new design direction, adapting UiPath style to a product surface, or reviewing whether an artifact feels on-brand.
6. Use [references/example-images.md](references/example-images.md) when visual examples would help match proportions, color blocking, type scale, pixel usage, or document/report composition.
7. Use [references/source/uipath-brand-book-v3.3.pdf](references/source/uipath-brand-book-v3.3.pdf) as the bundled source reference when the condensed notes are insufficient or the user asks for source-grounded brand interpretation.
8. Apply semantic brand choices:
   - Use Robotic Orange for robots, impact, CTAs, headlines, and key highlights.
   - Use Agentic Teal for agents and AI-agent concepts.
   - Use Bright White as canvas and breathing room.
   - Use Deep Blue for brand-related structure and contrast.
   - Use Black for platform-specific content or people-related contrast.
9. Validate against the review checklist in [references/design-language.md](references/design-language.md) before finalizing.

## Core Principles

- Be human, direct, non-jargony, warm, and skimmable. Lead with audience benefit, then support with details.
- Build around confident type, intentional white space, strong contrast, and modular pixel-based structure.
- Treat pixels as meaningful units of orchestration and automation, not decorative confetti.
- Use color for hierarchy and meaning. Secondary and tertiary colors support; they should not compete with primary colors.
- Avoid generic tech visuals, over-staged photography, cluttered crops, off-palette accents, and layouts that feel like a Tetris grid.
- Do not invent or modify logo lockups, avatars, awards, or official assets. Use approved files and route custom needs through UiPath Creative Studio or Brand Center.

## Practical Defaults

- Use Poppins Bold for everyday headlines when available; use Inter or Arial as practical fallbacks depending on medium.
- Use Inter Regular for body copy in digital work; use Arial for PowerPoint and shared-office contexts unless the official template provides other settings.
- Use short CTAs of two or three words. Prefer white on Robotic Orange, Robotic Orange on white, black on Agentic Teal, or white outlines on dark backgrounds.
- For UI or product mockups, use restrained UiPath expression: strong hierarchy, clear grid, selective orange/teal highlights, and small pixel motifs. Do not let brand decoration reduce usability.
- For campaign or event creative, allow stronger blocks, oversized type, high-energy color, gradients, and pixel systems, but keep message hierarchy dominant.
- If a higher-priority instruction or target platform disallows a Brand Book setting, preserve the intent with the nearest compliant choice and state the tradeoff.

## Asset Handling

This skill bundles the source brand book PDF, rendered visual examples, official UiPath corporate logo files, and the provided white/dark-grey 96 px SVG icon libraries. It does not bundle separate official fonts, templates, Otto artwork, glyph sets, lockups, or other Brand Center production assets. When an output requires assets not bundled here, use already-approved assets supplied by the user or direct the workflow to the UiPath Brand Center or Creative Studio.
